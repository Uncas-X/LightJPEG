LightJPEG includes MozJPEG 4.1.5 (Mozilla JPEG Encoder Project).
https://github.com/mozilla/mozjpeg/tree/v4.1.5

This software is based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.

The bundled cjpeg executable is built from the unmodified official v4.1.5
source release, with the JPEG library linked statically. SIMD, libpng support,
the TurboJPEG API and shared-library builds are disabled. Image decoding and
colour conversion are performed by Apple's system ImageIO and CoreGraphics.
No Homebrew or other third-party dynamic libraries are needed at runtime.

The relevant copyright notices, licence terms and disclaimers are reproduced
in MozJPEG-LICENSE.md and MozJPEG-README.ijg in this directory. The complete
unmodified upstream source, including file-level copyright notices, accompanies
the LightJPEG source distribution in vendor/mozjpeg-4.1.5.

When the included archive is present, rebuilding requires no network access.
